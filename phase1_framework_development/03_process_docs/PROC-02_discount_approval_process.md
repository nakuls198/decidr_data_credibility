# PROC-02 — Discount Approval Process

- **Owner:** Victor Adeyemi, Financial Controller
- **Last updated:** 2022-08-15
- **Related policy:** POL-01

## Purpose

Ensure discounts above the approval threshold are reviewed before a deal closes.

## Trigger

Account Executive prepares a deal with a discount at or above the policy threshold.

## Steps

1. Account Executive flags the deal as requiring approval in the CRM.
2. Account Executive submits discount rationale (competitive pressure, deal size, strategic account, etc.) to the Financial Controller.
3. **Financial Controller reviews and approves or rejects the discount**, recording the decision in the CRM `approval_record` field.
4. If approved, Account Executive proceeds to close the deal.
5. If rejected, Account Executive renegotiates terms with the customer.

## Responsible / Accountable

- **Responsible:** Account Executive (steps 1, 2, 4/5)
- **Accountable:** Financial Controller (step 3)

## Inputs

CRM deal record, discount rationale.

## Outputs

Recorded approval/rejection decision.

## Systems used

CRM.

## Required approvals

Financial Controller sign-off (per this document).

## Expected timing

1 business day.

## Exceptions and escalation path

Not documented.
