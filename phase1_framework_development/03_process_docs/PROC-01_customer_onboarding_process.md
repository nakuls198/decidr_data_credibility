# PROC-01 — Customer Onboarding & Implementation Process

- **Owner:** Helena Cho, Head of Customer Success & Delivery
- **Last updated:** 2024-01-20
- **Related policy:** POL-03

## Purpose

Take a newly signed customer from contract signature to productive use of Northfield OS within 6 weeks.

## Trigger

Deal marked "Closed Won" in CRM.

## Steps

1. **Assignment** (Day 0–2): Implementation Specialist assigned by Head of Customer Success & Delivery, based on current workload.
2. **Kickoff call** (Week 1): Specialist runs kickoff call with customer's nominated admin; confirms scope, systems access, data migration needs.
3. **Configuration** (Weeks 1–3): Specialist configures Northfield OS for customer's team structure, imports existing scheduling data.
4. **Training** (Weeks 3–4): Specialist runs live training sessions for customer's field staff and office admin.
5. **Go-live** (Week 5): Customer begins using Northfield OS for live scheduling.
6. **Handover to Support** (Week 6): Specialist marks onboarding complete in project system; account formally handed to the Support team via handover checklist.

## Responsible / Accountable

- **Responsible:** Assigned Implementation Specialist (all steps)
- **Accountable:** Head of Customer Success & Delivery

## Inputs

Signed contract, customer's existing data/spreadsheets (if migrating), CRM deal record.

## Outputs

Configured Northfield OS instance, trained customer users, completed onboarding checklist in project system, invoicing triggered.

## Systems used

CRM, project system, Northfield OS admin console.

## Required approvals

None specified for standard onboarding. Timeline extensions for complex accounts are handled case-by-case (no formal approval step documented).

## Expected timing

6 weeks, start to finish.

## Exceptions and escalation path

Not documented. In practice, onboarding-related technical issues are raised directly with Engineering rather than through a documented escalation step (see PROC-05 for the general escalation path).
