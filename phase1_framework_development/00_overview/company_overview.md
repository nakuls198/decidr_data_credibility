# Northfield Software Ltd — Company Overview

*Student-facing document. Part of Phase 1 release.*

## What Northfield does

Northfield Software Ltd ("Northfield") builds and sells **Northfield OS**, a scheduling, compliance-tracking and invoicing product for small and mid-sized trades and facilities-services businesses (electrical, HVAC, plumbing, commercial cleaning). Customers are typically businesses with 5–200 field staff.

Northfield sells directly (no channel/reseller layer), with a small implementation team that onboards new customers and a support team that handles ongoing issues. It is privately held, founded in 2020, and has never raised outside venture funding beyond an early seed round.

## Size and structure

- **29 employees** as of 31 July 2026.
- **Five business functions**: Leadership, Sales, Customer Success & Delivery, Engineering & Product, Finance & People Ops.
- Single office location; fully in-person with two remote engineers.

## Dataset period

The dataset covers **1 August 2025 – 31 July 2026** ("the period"). Some documents (policies, role descriptions) predate the period and remain in force; where a document has been superseded, both versions are provided, as they would be in a real organisation.

All claims, evidence and records should be read as current **as at 31 July 2026** unless a document states otherwise.

## Reading this dataset

This dataset is **deliberately imperfect**. It contains gaps, contradictions, outdated documents, claims supported by only one source, and areas where the organisation itself does not have a settled answer. This is intentional and is the subject of the assessment exercise — do not assume any single document is authoritative simply because it looks formal, recent, or comes from a senior person.
