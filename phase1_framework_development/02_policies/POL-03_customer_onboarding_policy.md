# POL-03 — Customer Onboarding & Implementation Policy

- **Document owner:** Helena Cho, Head of Customer Success & Delivery
- **Version:** 1.4
- **Approval status:** Approved
- **Effective date:** 2024-01-15
- **Review date:** 2026-01-15
- **Supersedes:** v1.0–1.3 (minor wording revisions only; no substantive change to timeline or ownership since v1.0)
- **Applicable to:** All new-business customer accounts
- **Related documents:** PROC-01 (Customer Onboarding & Implementation Process), PROC-06 (Invoicing & Collections Process)

## Purpose

To ensure new customers reach productive use of Northfield OS within a predictable timeframe, with clear ownership.

## Formal requirements

1. Every new-business account is assigned a named Implementation Specialist within 2 business days of contract signature.
2. Standard onboarding is completed within **6 weeks** of assignment.
3. The assigned Implementation Specialist is accountable for onboarding completion for their account, including coordinating any input required from Support or Engineering.
4. Onboarding is considered complete only when the account's onboarding checklist is marked complete in the project system.
5. Formal handover to the Support team must occur before an account is considered to be in standard support.

## Exceptions

Timeline extensions may be granted for accounts with unusual technical complexity. This policy does not specify who holds authority to approve such an extension.

## Background

Prior to this policy, onboarding was managed on an ad hoc basis by whichever Account Executive closed the deal, with no dedicated implementation role or standard timeline.
