# POL-04 — Support Escalation Policy (Revision)

- **Document owner:** Sam Okonkwo, Support Lead
- **Version:** 2.0 — DRAFT
- **Approval status:** DRAFT — not approved; informal pilot proposed, see MTG-04.
- **Effective date:** Not yet in force
- **Review date:** N/A (pending approval decision)
- **Applicable to:** Support Reps and Support Lead (Customer Success & Delivery); on-call Engineering staff, as the escalation counterparty
- **Related documents:** PROC-05 (Support Escalation Process, v1 — still the only approved version)

## Purpose (as drafted)

To revise the escalation path so that Severity 1 and 2 issues can be escalated directly to a named on-call engineer via Slack, rather than requiring a ticket to be raised first.

## Proposed requirements (NOT formally in force)

1. Severity 1 issues may be escalated directly to the on-call engineer via the `#eng-oncall` Slack channel. A ticket should be raised retrospectively within 24 hours.
2. Severity 2 issues continue to require a ticket before escalation, as under the current (v1) process.
3. Support Reps may close an escalated ticket once the customer confirms resolution, without requiring Engineering sign-off.

## Exceptions (as drafted)

None proposed beyond the Severity 1 Slack-escalation path itself, which departs from the standard ticket-first process described in PROC-05.
