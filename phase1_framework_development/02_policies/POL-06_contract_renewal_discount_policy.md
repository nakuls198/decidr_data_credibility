# POL-06 — Contract Renewal Discount Policy

- **Document owner:** Diane Okafor, Head of Sales
- **Version:** 1.1
- **Approval status:** Approved
- **Effective date:** 2024-06-01
- **Review date:** 2026-06-01 (review not yet conducted as of period end)
- **Applicable to:** All contract renewal negotiations
- **Related documents:** PROC-07 (Contract Renewal & Churn-Risk Review Process), POL-01 (Discount Approval Policy — new business only)

## Purpose

To set consistent expectations for discounting at renewal, separate from new-business discounting under POL-01.

## Formal requirements

1. Renewal discounts are capped at **10%** off the account's current contracted rate.
2. No exceptions to the renewal discount cap are provided for under this policy.
3. Renewal discount decisions are made by the Account Executive responsible for the account, in consultation with Customer Success on relationship health.

## Exceptions

None documented in this version.
