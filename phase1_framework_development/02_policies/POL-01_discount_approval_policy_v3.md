# POL-01 — Discount Approval Policy

- **Document owner:** Harriet Doyle, Head of Finance & People Ops
- **Version:** 3.0
- **Approval status:** Approved
- **Effective date:** 2025-03-01
- **Review date:** 2026-09-01
- **Supersedes:** POL-02 (v2.1, effective 2021-06-01 – 2025-02-28)
- **Applicable to:** All employees with deal-closing authority (Sales function)
- **Related documents:** PROC-02 (Discount Approval Process), POL-06 (Contract Renewal Discount Policy)

## Purpose

To ensure discounts offered to customers are commercially sound and do not erode margin without appropriate oversight.

## Formal requirements

1. Any discount of **15% or greater** off list price on a new-business deal requires written approval from the Head of Finance & People Ops before the deal is marked Closed Won in the CRM.
2. Approval must be recorded in the CRM's `approval_record` field, referencing the approver and date.
3. Discounts below 15% may be approved by the deal's Account Executive without further sign-off.
4. This policy applies to new-business deals. Renewal discounting is governed separately by POL-06.

## Exceptions

None are formally documented under this version. Any deviation from the above requires an updated version of this policy.
