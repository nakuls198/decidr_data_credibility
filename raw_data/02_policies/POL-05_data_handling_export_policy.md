# POL-05 — Data Handling & Customer Data Export Policy

- **Document owner:** Paul Vasquez, Office & IT Ops Coordinator
- **Version:** 1.2
- **Approval status:** Approved
- **Effective date:** 2023-05-01
- **Review date:** 2025-05-01 (review overdue by more than a year as of period end)
- **Applicable to:** All employees with access to customer data
- **Related documents:** None formally linked.

## Purpose

To set expectations for how employees handle customer data outside of Northfield's core systems.

## Formal requirements

1. Customer data should primarily be accessed and worked with inside approved systems (CRM, project system, Northfield OS admin console).
2. Where offline work is genuinely required, customer data **may be exported to a personal device for the purposes of offline work, provided it is deleted within 24 hours.**
3. Customer data must not be shared externally without the customer's consent.

## Exceptions

None documented.
