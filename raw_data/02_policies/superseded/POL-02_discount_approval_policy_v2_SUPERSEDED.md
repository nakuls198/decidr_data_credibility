## Purpose

To ensure discounts offered to customers are commercially sound and do not erode margin without appropriate oversight.

## Formal requirements

1. Any discount of **10% or greater** off list price requires written approval from the Head of Finance & People Ops before the deal is marked Closed Won.
2. Approval must be recorded in the CRM's `approval_record` field.
3. Discounts below 10% may be approved by the deal's Account Executive without further sign-off.

## Exceptions

None documented.
