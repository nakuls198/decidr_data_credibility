# POL-07 — Expense Approval Policy

- **Document owner:** Harriet Doyle, Head of Finance & People Ops
- **Version:** 2.0
- **Approval status:** Approved
- **Effective date:** 2021-09-01
- **Review date:** 2026-09-01
- **Applicable to:** All employees submitting expense claims
- **Related documents:** PROC-04 (Expense Approval Process)

## Purpose

To ensure business expenses are reviewed and approved consistently before reimbursement.

## Formal requirements

1. Expense claims **over $500** require approval from the Head of Finance & People Ops before reimbursement is processed.
2. Expense claims of $500 or less may be approved by the employee's direct manager.
3. All expense claims must include an itemised receipt.

## Exceptions

None documented in this version.
