# MTG-06 — Leadership Strategy Session

- **Date:** 2026-01-20
- **Participants:** Renata Voss (CEO), Marcus Ihejirika (COO), Diane Okafor (Head of Sales), Lior Ben-David (Head of Engineering), Harriet Doyle (Head of Finance & People Ops)
- **Purpose:** Review progress on evaluating expansion into the commercial cleaning services vertical (a market-adjacency exploration started in mid-2025).

## Discussion

Diane presented early customer discovery findings from conversations with cleaning-services businesses: interest existed but sales cycles looked longer and the compliance-tracking features needed differed meaningfully from the trades-focused product. Lior flagged that supporting the vertical properly would likely require a dedicated engineering workstream, competing with the current roadmap. Renata noted the company did not currently have bandwidth to run two go-to-market motions in parallel.

Renata: *"I don't think we say never to this, but I don't think now is the moment either. Let's park it — not kill it, just park it — and revisit if the trades pipeline slows down or if we get a clear inbound signal from the cleaning side."*

## Options considered

1. Proceed with a dedicated cleaning-vertical go-to-market effort in 2026.
2. Pause the exploration indefinitely.
3. Pause the exploration with no defined trigger to revisit.

## Final decision

Pause the cleaning-vertical exploration. Renata mentioned a loose trigger for revisiting it verbally, but nothing more specific than that was written down anywhere.

## Decision owner

Renata Voss (CEO).

## Rationale

Limited bandwidth to run two go-to-market motions simultaneously; deprioritise the less mature opportunity.

## Conditions or exceptions

Renata's verbal framing ("revisit if the trades pipeline slows down or if we get a clear inbound signal") was not translated into any tracked metric, owner, or review date.

## Actions

- Diane Okafor to file discovery notes (informal, not distributed beyond this group as far as records show).
- No one was assigned to monitor for the "revisit" trigger Renata described.

## Unresolved matters

No later leadership meeting record revisited this decision.
