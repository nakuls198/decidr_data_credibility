# MTG-01 — Discount Policy Review

- **Date:** 2025-02-24
- **Participants:** Renata Voss (CEO), Harriet Doyle (Head of Finance & People Ops), Diane Okafor (Head of Sales)
- **Purpose:** Review whether the 10% discount approval threshold is still appropriate given deal-size trends.

## Discussion

Harriet presented data showing that a growing share of deals were being flagged for approval at the 10% threshold, adding review burden without changing outcomes (almost all were approved). Diane argued the threshold was out of step with typical enterprise-software discounting norms and was slowing deals down unnecessarily. Renata asked whether raising the threshold created margin risk; Harriet said modelling suggested minimal impact given historical discount patterns clustered below 15%.

## Options considered

1. Keep threshold at 10%.
2. Raise threshold to 15%.
3. Raise threshold to 20%.

## Final decision

Raise the Finance approval threshold from 10% to 15%, effective 2025-03-01. Option 3 (20%) was discussed but not adopted — Harriet felt it removed too much oversight.

## Decision owner

Harriet Doyle (policy owner), with Renata Voss's sign-off.

## Rationale

Reduce approval overhead on low-risk deals while retaining oversight on larger discounts.

None discussed or agreed.
