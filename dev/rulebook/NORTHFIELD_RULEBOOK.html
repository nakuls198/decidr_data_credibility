Northfield Rulebook

Cross-reference map on top of Data Atlas. For each process: linked files, supersession, and which source wins by dispute type. Built from documents.jsonl, the claims register, and Kai/Bianca guidance (CRM for occurrence; no single-variable formality).

1. Dispute winners (always)

RULE

What is the written rule?

  • Winner: approved in-force policy
  • Demote: superseded, draft, stale process

HAPPENED

Was it recorded?

  • Winner: CRM (Kai)
  • Demote: Slack/interview vs CRM field

PRACTICE

What do people do?

  • Winner: meeting + actors + CRM exceptions
  • Copied Slack = one source

GAP

Is anyone assigned?

  • If decision-rights empty → gap
  • Do not invent an owner
