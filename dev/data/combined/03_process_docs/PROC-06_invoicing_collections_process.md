# PROC-06 — Invoicing & Collections Process

- **Owner:** Victor Adeyemi, Financial Controller
- **Last updated:** 2022-11-01
- **Related policy:** None formal; operates alongside POL-03.

## Purpose

Ensure customers are invoiced accurately and promptly, and overdue invoices are followed up.

## Trigger

CRM deal marked "Closed Won" AND linked onboarding project marked complete in the project system.

## Steps

1. Billing integration automatically generates an invoice in Northfield's finance system once both trigger conditions are met.
2. Invoice sent to customer's billing contact.
3. Financial Controller reviews payment status weekly.
4. If an invoice is overdue by 15 days, an automated reminder is sent.
5. If overdue by 30 days, Financial Controller personally contacts the customer.
6. If overdue by 45 days, Financial Controller escalates to the Head of Finance & People Ops for a decision on further action (late fee, account hold, or write-off).

## Responsible / Accountable

- **Responsible:** Financial Controller (steps 3–6), billing integration (steps 1–2, automated)
- **Accountable:** Financial Controller

## Inputs

CRM deal status, onboarding completion status, contract terms.

## Outputs

Issued invoice, payment record, collections escalation where relevant.

## Systems used

CRM, project system, Northfield finance system, billing integration between Northfield OS and CRM.

## Required approvals

Head of Finance & People Ops decision required at the 45-day escalation point.

## Expected timing

Invoice generated within 1 business day of both trigger conditions being met.

## Exceptions and escalation path

The 45-day escalation path is the only documented exception path.
