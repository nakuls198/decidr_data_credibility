## Discussion notes

On the billing integration: *"I built that integration back in 2021 and I'm still the only one who really understands how it talks to the CRM — nobody else has touched the production code for it since. It's held up fine day to day, but it's obviously a risk if I'm not around."*

On the February 2026 incident: *"The integration broke during a CRM update in February and stopped reconciling invoice status properly for about two days. We said afterwards we'd get proper documentation written up so it wasn't just in my head — Femi was going to lead that. Last I checked, it still hasn't happened. Not for any bad reason, just other things kept taking priority."*

On being pulled into incidents: *"During that incident people were messaging me directly rather than going through a ticket first — which makes sense given the urgency, but it does mean there's not always a clean paper trail of exactly what I changed and when, versus what showed up properly in the ticket afterwards."*

On feature intake: *"We've been in Linear for over a year now, not Trello — I think the last time I saw the old process doc it still mentioned Trello, which made me raise an eyebrow, but nobody's gotten around to updating it."*

On Product/Engineering prioritisation: *"Grace and Owen basically just divide up requests between them by who has bandwidth or knows the customer, there's no strict rule I've seen. As for who has the final say between Product and Engineering when we disagree on something — I genuinely don't know that there's ever been a clean answer to that. It just gets talked out."*

## Exceptions and workarounds
