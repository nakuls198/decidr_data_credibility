# PROC-03 — Product Change / Feature Request Intake Process

- **Owner:** Grace Whitfield, Product Manager
- **Last updated:** 2023-11-02
- **Related policy:** None formal.

## Purpose

Capture, triage and prioritise feature and product change requests from customers, Support and internal stakeholders.

## Trigger

Any stakeholder identifies a desired product change.

## Steps

1. Requester adds a card to the **Feature Requests Trello board**, tagging the relevant customer(s) if applicable.
2. Product Manager reviews new cards weekly and tags each with a priority (P1–P4).
3. Product Manager and Head of Engineering jointly review P1 cards in the weekly product-eng sync.
4. Approved cards are moved to the "Planned" column and assigned to an engineering sprint.
5. Requester is notified when their request ships.

## Responsible / Accountable

- **Responsible:** Requester (step 1), Product Manager (steps 2, 5)
- **Accountable:** Product Manager (prioritisation), Head of Engineering (feasibility/sequencing)

## Inputs

Customer feedback, Support tickets, internal ideas.

## Outputs

Prioritised, planned feature work.

## Systems used

Trello.

## Required approvals

Head of Engineering sign-off for P1 cards entering a sprint.

## Expected timing

Weekly triage cycle.

## Exceptions and escalation path

Not documented.
