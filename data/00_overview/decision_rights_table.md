# Decision Rights Table

*Student-facing document. Part of Phase 1 release. A cross-role summary of who holds which decision right, per the brief's request for org chart + role descriptions + a standalone decision-rights table as three distinct artifacts. Detail and caveats for each role are in [role_descriptions.md](role_descriptions.md); this table is the quick-reference cut across roles.*

| Decision | Formal decision right (documented) | Observed in practice | Documented? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Approve discount >15% on new-business deals | Head of Finance & People Ops (POL-01) | Often Head of Sales, informally, for urgent deals | Formal right documented; practice is not |
| Approve renewal discount | Account Executive, within the 10% cap (POL-06) | Same, but at least one instance of an undocumented exception above the cap | Formal right documented; exception is not |
| Final call on renewal terms when Sales and Customer Success disagree | **Not assigned to any role** | Resolved informally case-by-case, or left unresolved | Not documented — this is a genuine gap, not an omission from this table |
| Approve expense claims >$500 | Head of Finance & People Ops (POL-07) | Sometimes pre-approved informally by the employee's manager | Formal right documented; practice is not |
| Approve a deviation from the standard 6-week onboarding timeline | **Not formally assigned** — informally treated as the Head of Customer Success & Delivery's call | Same, by habit rather than documented authority | Not documented |
| Sign off on production deployments touching customer billing | Head of Engineering (per role description) | Mostly followed; one emergency fix during the February 2026 incident has no clear sign-off record | Formal right documented; one instance ambiguous |
| Approve a new hire's completion of probation | People Ops Lead, sole approver (POL-08) | Consistently followed | Documented and followed |
| Approve/reject a discount-relevant CRM stage change | Account Executive (deal owner) | Consistently followed | Documented and followed |
| Prioritise incoming feature requests | Product Managers jointly (PROC-03) | Split informally by bandwidth/customer familiarity, no fixed rule | Formal right documented; split method is not |
| Decide technical feasibility vs. prioritisation split between Engineering and Product | **Not documented anywhere** as a formal split | Assumed/talked out case-by-case | Not documented |
| Approve escalation-process changes (e.g. the v2 draft) | Head of Customer Success & Delivery | A pilot was authorised verbally; no formal approval was ever recorded, though the team has operated as if it were approved | Ambiguous — a decision right exists, but whether it was exercised is contested |
| Block a release for a data-quality defect | **Not documented** | QA Lead has flagged issues; no confirmed instance of unilaterally blocking a release | Not documented |

**How to read this table:** the gap between "Formal decision right" and "Observed in practice" is often the point of a specific reference claim (see the hidden reference set once released) — this table exists to make those gaps visible at a glance rather than requiring a read of every role description and process document to spot them.
