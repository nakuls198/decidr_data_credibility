# Role Descriptions and Decision Rights

*Student-facing document. Part of Phase 1 release. Covers roles most relevant to the core processes; not every role in the roster has a full description. See [decision_rights_table.md](decision_rights_table.md) for a cross-role decision-rights summary.*

## CEO — Renata Voss

- **Function:** Leadership
- **Reports to:** Board
- **Responsibilities:** Overall strategy, board relationship, final call on strategic decisions (market expansion, fundraising).
- **Decision rights:** Sole authority on strategic direction; delegates day-to-day operational authority to the COO and function heads.
- **Systems accessed:** All systems (admin access).
- **Process participation:** P7 (strategic escalations only), general oversight.
- **Backup/delegate:** COO acts with delegated authority in the CEO's absence, except for strategic decisions.

## COO — Marcus Ihejirika

- **Function:** Leadership
- **Reports to:** Renata Voss (CEO)
- **Responsibilities:** Operational oversight across Customer Success & Delivery, cross-functional coordination.
- **Decision rights:** Formal line management of Head of Customer Success & Delivery. In practice, day-to-day CS&D decisions are made directly by the Head of Customer Success & Delivery without routine COO involvement (see interviews and meeting records).
- **Systems accessed:** CRM, project system (read access).
- **Process participation:** P2, P8 (formal oversight; limited direct involvement observed).
- **Backup/delegate:** None named.

## Head of Sales — Diane Okafor

- **Function:** Sales
- **Reports to:** Renata Voss (CEO)
- **Responsibilities:** Sales strategy, team management, pipeline oversight, deal approvals within her authority.
- **Decision rights:** Approves Account Executive assignment, sales targets. Discount approval authority above 15% formally sits with the Head of Finance & People Ops, not Sales — see POL-01.
- **Systems accessed:** CRM (full access).
- **Process participation:** P1 (requester side), P8 (joint accountability with Head of CS&D — no documented tie-breaker).
- **Backup/delegate:** Callum Reyes (Senior Account Executive) informally covers for Diane on deal matters when she is unavailable; this is not a documented delegation.

## Senior Account Executive — Callum Reyes

- **Function:** Sales
- **Reports to:** Diane Okafor (Head of Sales)
- **Responsibilities:** Manage largest/most strategic accounts, mentor newer AEs.
- **Decision rights:** Standard AE discount authority below 15% (POL-01).
- **Systems accessed:** CRM.
- **Process participation:** P1, P8.

## Head of Customer Success & Delivery — Helena Cho

- **Function:** Customer Success & Delivery
- **Reports to:** Marcus Ihejirika (COO)
- **Responsibilities:** Onboarding delivery, support quality, renewal/churn-risk input, team management across Implementation and Support.
- **Decision rights:** Accountable for onboarding process (POL-03). No documented sole decision right over renewal terms — shared with Head of Sales (see PROC-07).
- **Systems accessed:** CRM, project system, support ticketing system.
- **Process participation:** P2, P5, P8.
- **Backup/delegate:** None named; several critical decisions (e.g. escalation-process changes) rest with her alone with no deputy identified.

## Support Lead — Sam Okonkwo

- **Function:** Customer Success & Delivery
- **Reports to:** Helena Cho (Head of Customer Success & Delivery)
- **Responsibilities:** Day-to-day support team management, ticket triage, escalation to Engineering.
- **Decision rights:** Accountable for ticket lifecycle under PROC-05. Proposed the draft v2 escalation revision (POL-04), which has not been formally approved.
- **Systems accessed:** Support ticketing system, CRM (read access).
- **Process participation:** P5.

## Head of Engineering — Lior Ben-David

- **Function:** Engineering & Product
- **Reports to:** Renata Voss (CEO)
- **Responsibilities:** Engineering delivery, technical architecture decisions, production deployment sign-off.
- **Decision rights:** Final say on technical feasibility for feature requests (see PROC-03; no single document states the Product/Engineering prioritisation split explicitly). **Production deployments touching customer billing require his sign-off** before release.
- **Systems accessed:** All engineering and production systems.
- **Process participation:** P3, P5 (engineering escalation), P7 (billing integration dependency).
- **Backup/delegate:** No named delegate for deployment sign-off.

## Product Managers — Grace Whitfield, Owen Mbeki

- **Function:** Engineering & Product
- **Reports to:** Lior Ben-David (Head of Engineering)
- **Responsibilities:** Feature request triage and prioritisation (PROC-03).
- **Decision rights:** Prioritisation call, per PROC-03. The two PMs split incoming requests informally; no documented rule for how product areas are divided between them.
- **Systems accessed:** Linear (current), CRM (read access).
- **Process participation:** P3.

## Senior Engineer — Ana Kowalski

## QA Lead — Sofia Marchetti

- **Reports to:** Lior Ben-David (Head of Engineering)

- **Decision rights:** Not formally documented with respect to blocking a release. Her role description predates the current release process and does not address release-blocking authority.

## Head of Finance & People Ops — Harriet Doyle

- **Function:** Finance & People Ops
- **Reports to:** Renata Voss (CEO)
- **Responsibilities:** Finance, expense approval, discount approval oversight, people operations oversight.
- **Decision rights:** Formal discount approval authority above 15% (POL-01), formal expense approval authority above $500 (POL-07).
- **Systems accessed:** CRM (approval fields), finance system, expenses spreadsheet.
- **Process participation:** P1, P4, P7.
- **Backup/delegate:** No named backup approver; in practice, Head of Sales exercises informal discount-approval authority in Harriet's absence or for urgent deals (undocumented).
