# POL-08 — New Hire Onboarding & Probation Policy

- **Document owner:** Naomi Kessler, People Ops Lead
- **Version:** 1.3
- **Approval status:** Approved
- **Effective date:** 2021-05-24
- **Review date:** 2026-05-24
- **Applicable to:** All new employees
- **Related documents:** None formally linked.

## Purpose

To ensure new hires are set up consistently and that probation outcomes are decided by a single accountable role.

## Formal requirements

1. Every new hire completes a documented onboarding checklist (systems access, policy acknowledgement, role-specific training) within their **first two weeks**.
2. The People Ops Lead is the **sole approver** of a new hire's successful completion of probation, based on input from the hire's manager.
3. Probation period is 3 months from start date.

## Exceptions

This policy does not address onboarding for remote employees specifically; the checklist was written with an in-person office setup assumed.

## Background

Before this policy was introduced, new-hire onboarding was handled informally by whichever manager was doing the hiring, with no standard checklist and no single approver of probation outcomes.
