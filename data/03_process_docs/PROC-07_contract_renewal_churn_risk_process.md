# PROC-07 — Contract Renewal & Churn-Risk Review Process

- **Owner:** Jointly maintained by Sales and Customer Success & Delivery
- **Last updated:** 2024-07-05
- **Related policy:** POL-06

## Purpose

Identify accounts at risk of not renewing, and manage the renewal negotiation process.

## Trigger

Account reaches 90 days before contract end date, OR Customer Success flags an account as churn-risk based on usage/engagement signals.

## Steps

1. Customer Success reviews account health quarterly and flags at-risk accounts.
2. Flagged accounts are discussed at the joint Sales/CS renewal review meeting.
3. Account Executive leads commercial renewal conversation with the customer.
4. Customer Success provides relationship-health input to the Account Executive during negotiation.
5. Renewal terms (including any discount, per POL-06) are agreed and the contract is renewed or the account churns.

## Responsible / Accountable

- **Responsible:** Customer Success (flagging, relationship input), Account Executive (commercial negotiation)
- **Accountable:** Not specified — see Exceptions.

## Inputs

Account usage data, contract terms, relationship history.

## Outputs

Renewed contract or confirmed churn.

## Systems used

CRM, Northfield OS usage analytics (informal review, not a defined dashboard).

## Required approvals

Discount terms per POL-06. No approval step specified for the renewal decision itself.

## Expected timing

Review cycle begins 90 days before contract end date.

## Exceptions and escalation path

Not documented. Both the Head of Sales and Head of Customer Success & Delivery have historically signed off on renewal strategy for at-risk accounts, but no escalation path exists for cases where they disagree on approach or discount terms.
