# PROC-04 — Expense Approval Process

- **Owner:** Harriet Doyle, Head of Finance & People Ops
- **Last updated:** 2021-09-10
- **Related policy:** POL-07

## Purpose

Review and approve employee expense claims prior to reimbursement.

## Trigger

Employee submits an expense claim with receipt(s).

## Steps

1. Employee submits claim via the shared expenses spreadsheet, attaching receipts.
2. If claim is $500 or under, employee's direct manager reviews and approves.
3. If claim is over $500, claim is routed to the Head of Finance & People Ops for approval.
4. Approved claims are processed for reimbursement in the next pay cycle.

## Responsible / Accountable

- **Responsible:** Employee (step 1), direct manager or Head of Finance & People Ops (approval step)
- **Accountable:** Head of Finance & People Ops

## Inputs

Expense claim, itemised receipts.

## Outputs

Approved/rejected claim, reimbursement.

## Systems used

Shared expenses spreadsheet.

## Required approvals

Per POL-07 thresholds.

## Expected timing

Within the same pay cycle the claim is submitted, where submitted before the monthly cutoff.

## Exceptions and escalation path

Not documented. (Travel expenses incurred during customer onboarding visits are treated by Finance as standing pre-approved in practice, but this exception does not appear in this process document or in POL-07.)
