# PROC-05 — Support Escalation Process (v1 — approved)

- **Owner:** Sam Okonkwo, Support Lead
- **Last updated:** 2021-02-10
- **Approval status:** Approved. This is the current formally approved version. (See POL-04 for an unapproved draft revision.)

## Purpose

Ensure customer-reported issues that Support cannot resolve are escalated to Engineering consistently.

## Trigger

Support Rep cannot resolve a customer-reported issue within their access/knowledge.

## Steps

1. Support Rep classifies severity (Sev 1–3) based on customer impact.
2. Support Rep raises a ticket in the support system, tagging severity and affected system.
3. Support Lead reviews Sev 1 tickets immediately; Sev 2/3 tickets at next triage.
4. Ticket assigned to an engineer by the Head of Engineering (or delegate).
5. Engineer investigates, resolves or escalates further, and updates the ticket.
6. Support Lead confirms resolution with the customer and closes the ticket.

## Responsible / Accountable

- **Responsible:** Support Rep (steps 1–2), assigned Engineer (step 5)
- **Accountable:** Support Lead (ticket lifecycle), Head of Engineering (engineering assignment)

## Inputs

Customer report, product/system knowledge.

## Outputs

Resolved ticket, customer communication.

## Systems used

Support ticketing system, Slack (internal coordination only).

## Required approvals

None for standard resolution; Support Lead confirms closure.

## Expected timing

Sev 1: same business day. Sev 2: 2 business days. Sev 3: 5 business days.

## Exceptions and escalation path

None documented for bypassing the ticket step. In practice, Sev 1 production issues are frequently raised directly with an engineer via Slack before or instead of a ticket being created.
