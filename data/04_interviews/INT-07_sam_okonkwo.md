## Discussion notes

On the escalation process revision: *"We've basically been running on the new version since last September — direct-to-oncall for Sev 1s, reps can close out tickets themselves once the customer's confirmed it's fixed. Helena signed off on that at the time."*

On reps closing tickets: *"My reps can close a ticket themselves once the customer's confirmed the fix is working — they don't need to loop Engineering back in for that."*

## Exceptions and workarounds
