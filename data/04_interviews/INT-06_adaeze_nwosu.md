# INT-06 — Interview: Adaeze Nwosu

- **Role / function:** Implementation Specialist, Customer Success & Delivery
- **Tenure:** 4.2 years
- **Interview date:** 2026-06-19
- **Areas of responsibility:** Customer onboarding for assigned accounts.

## Processes performed directly

P2 (onboarding, full lifecycle), informal support of P5 during onboarding-adjacent issues.

## Processes only observed or heard about

P1, P4, P7, P8 — aware only peripherally.

## Discussion notes

On the onboarding process: *"Kickoff in week one, config and training over the next few weeks, go-live around week five, and then we hand off to Support in week six. That's basically always how it runs — no idea why six weeks specifically, that's just always been the number since before I joined."*

On onboarding effort not captured in the system: *"A good chunk of my week is just emailing customers back and forth, sorting out little things — none of that shows up as a task. If someone only looked at the project system they'd think onboarding takes way less effort than it does."*

On supporting newer colleagues: *"Ruth's still fairly new — I've walked her through a few onboardings that weren't quite textbook. I know at least once she was working off an older version of our checklist that one of the other Specialists had saved locally; I told her to use the current one, but I don't know how she originally got the old one."*

On travel: *"Nobody makes us get separate sign-off for onboarding-visit travel, we just book it. It's not written down that I know of, it's just accepted."*

On timeline extensions: *"If an account's more complex we might run a bit longer than six weeks. I've just always cleared that with Helena informally — I couldn't point you to anything that says it has to be her specifically."*

## Exceptions and workarounds

Timeline extensions handled informally; some onboarding effort untracked in the project system; outdated checklist circulating informally.

## Statements of uncertainty

Explicit on the "why six weeks" question and on whether Helena's sign-off is a formal requirement or just her own habit.
