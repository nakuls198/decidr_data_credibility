# MTG-05 — Quarterly Renewal & Churn-Risk Review

- **Date:** 2025-11-14
- **Participants:** Diane Okafor (Head of Sales), Helena Cho (Head of Customer Success & Delivery), Callum Reyes (Senior Account Executive)
- **Purpose:** Review accounts approaching renewal and identify churn-risk accounts requiring proactive outreach.

## Discussion

Helena presented usage data flagging four accounts as churn-risk based on declining login activity. The group agreed these should receive proactive outreach within 30 days. Discussion then turned to a specific enterprise account (referred to in this meeting as the "Ridgeway account") approaching contract end, where Callum (account owner) wanted to offer a discount above the 10% renewal cap to secure the renewal, citing competitive pressure from a rival vendor. Helena pushed back, arguing the relationship was strong enough that a large discount wasn't necessary and would set a bad precedent. Diane and Helena did not reach agreement on the discount level during the meeting.

Diane: *"I hear you, but ultimately if it's my rep's account and my number to hit, I think that's a sales call."*
Helena: *"I'm not saying it isn't, I just want it on record that I don't think it's necessary here."*

## Options considered

1. Offer the Ridgeway account a renewal discount above 10% to secure the deal.
2. Hold firm at the 10% cap and risk losing the account.
3. Delay the decision pending further customer conversation.

## Final decision

No decision was reached in this meeting. The group agreed to "keep talking to the customer" without committing to a discount figure. Neither Diane nor Helena deferred to the other, and no one else at the meeting had authority to break the tie.

## Decision owner

Not established. Neither role has documented final authority over renewal discount decisions when Sales and Customer Success disagree (see PROC-07).

## Rationale

N/A — no decision made.

## Conditions or exceptions

N/A.

## Actions

- Helena Cho and CS team to begin proactive outreach to the four flagged churn-risk accounts within 30 days.
- Callum Reyes to continue the Ridgeway conversation and report back. No due date was set for a final resolution.

## Unresolved matters

- The Ridgeway account's renewal discount was not resolved in this meeting or, as far as the available records show, subsequently — its contract end date passed without a formal extension or renewal being executed.
- No mechanism exists for resolving disagreements between Sales and Customer Success on renewal terms.
