# MTG-04 — Customer Success & Delivery Team Meeting

- **Date:** 2025-09-09
- **Participants:** Helena Cho (Head of Customer Success & Delivery), Sam Okonkwo (Support Lead), Ines Torres, Deshawn Riley (Support Reps)
- **Purpose:** Discuss proposed revisions to the support escalation process.

## Discussion

Sam presented a draft revision (subsequently circulated as POL-04 v2) allowing direct Slack escalation to an on-call engineer for Sev 1 issues, and allowing reps to close tickets themselves once a customer confirms resolution, without requiring Engineering sign-off. Sam argued the current ticket-first requirement was adding delay during production incidents when engineers were often being contacted directly anyway. Helena was broadly supportive but cautious about formalising a process change without more data.

Helena: *"I like the direction. Let's pilot it informally for a quarter — don't roll it out as official yet, just see how it goes — and then we'll look at formally updating the process document if it's working."*

## Options considered

1. Keep current (v1) process as-is.
2. Approve v2 immediately as the new standard.
3. Pilot v2 informally for one quarter before deciding.

## Final decision

Option 3 — an informal quarter-long pilot, rather than formal approval of the revised process document (POL-04).

## Decision owner

Helena Cho.

## Rationale

Test the revised approach before committing to a formal process change.

## Conditions or exceptions

Explicitly framed as a pilot, not a policy change. Helena did not set a specific date to review the pilot outcome or decide on formal approval.

## Actions

- Sam Okonkwo to run the pilot with his team starting immediately.
- No action item was created for Helena (or anyone) to formally review the pilot at the end of the quarter.

## Unresolved matters

The pilot review Helena mentioned doesn't appear to have happened. POL-04 is still sitting in draft, even though Sam's team has been running on it since September 2025.
