# MTG-10 — Ashgrove Trades Renewal Review

- **Date:** 2026-07-20
- **Participants:** Diane Okafor (Head of Sales), Helena Cho (Head of Customer Success & Delivery), Ruth Padgett (Implementation Specialist, account contact)
- **Purpose:** Finalise renewal terms for Ashgrove Trades ahead of its contract end date.

## Discussion

Ruth flagged that, in an earlier check-in call with the customer (not otherwise documented), she had told them informally that the renewal would "probably look similar to last time, around 10%" — a figure she'd never confirmed with Diane or Helena, just a reasonable guess based on the account's history. Diane said that assumption was now out of date: a competitor had recently approached Ashgrove Trades with an aggressive offer, and Diane wanted to secure a multi-year renewal before that went further. Helena agreed the relationship was strong enough to justify a modest increase in discount to lock in the term.

Diane: *"Ruth's 10% guess made sense at the time, but that's not where we should land now — I'd rather go to 12% and get two years locked in than hold the line and risk losing them to a competitive approach."*
Helena: *"Agreed. Let's make sure Ruth goes back to the customer with the actual number, not the placeholder one."*

## Options considered

1. Offer a one-year renewal at the previously-assumed ~10%, buying time before committing to anything longer.
2. Offer 12% in exchange for a two-year term.
3. Match the competitor's offer directly rather than counter with a round number.

## Final decision

Offer 12% for a two-year renewal term, in place of Ruth's earlier informal 10% figure, which had never been a documented or agreed position.

## Decision owner

Diane Okafor, with Helena Cho's agreement.

## Rationale

Competitive pressure and the value of locking in a longer term outweighed holding to the lower, informally-assumed figure.

## Conditions or exceptions

None beyond the terms above.
