# MTG-03 — Product/Engineering Planning

- **Date:** 2025-04-22
- **Participants:** Lior Ben-David (Head of Engineering), Grace Whitfield (Product Manager), Owen Mbeki (Product Manager), Ana Kowalski (Senior Engineer)
- **Purpose:** Address growing complaints that the Trello feature-request board was hard to search and didn't integrate with sprint planning.

## Discussion

Grace proposed migrating feature-request tracking to Linear, which the engineering team already used for sprint planning, to reduce duplicate tooling. Lior supported this; Ana noted it would simplify linking feature requests to engineering tickets directly. Owen raised that historical Trello cards would need to be manually re-entered or archived, which would take some cleanup time.

## Options considered

1. Keep Trello, improve labelling/process discipline.
2. Migrate fully to Linear.
3. Run both in parallel during a transition period.

## Final decision

Migrate fully to Linear. Historical open cards to be manually re-entered by end of April; Trello board archived (read-only) after that.

## Decision owner

Grace Whitfield (Product Manager), with Lior Ben-David's agreement on engineering-side implications.

## Rationale

Reduce duplicate tooling, improve traceability between feature requests and engineering delivery.

## Conditions or exceptions

None discussed regarding updating PROC-03 (the documented feature-intake process) to reflect the change — this was not raised as an action item.

## Actions

- Grace Whitfield to re-enter open Trello cards into Linear by 2025-04-30. (Completed — see TASK-140.)
- Owen Mbeki to archive the Trello board. (Completed.)
- No one was assigned to update PROC-03.
