# MTG-09 — Quarterly Finance & Ops Review

- **Date:** 2026-06-03
- **Participants:** Harriet Doyle (Head of Finance & People Ops), Victor Adeyemi (Financial Controller), Naomi Kessler (People Ops Lead)
- **Purpose:** Standing quarterly review of finance and operations matters.

## Discussion

Victor raised that several expense claims over the past quarter appeared to have been approved directly by managers rather than routed to Harriet, despite being under the informal understanding that manager approval only applied at or below $500. He cited two or three examples without pulling exact figures in the meeting. Harriet acknowledged this was a known pattern and said it hadn't caused any material issue so far, but agreed the policy or process should probably be tightened up, or at least clarified, so it was less ambiguous in practice. No specific changes were drafted in the meeting itself.

Harriet: *"Let's flag it for next quarter — I don't think we need to overreact, but I do want to look at whether the policy needs tightening or whether we just need to remind people how it's supposed to work."*

## Options considered

1. Leave as-is; treat as low-risk.
2. Update POL-07/PROC-04 to close the gap.
3. Reissue guidance reminding managers of the existing threshold, without changing the policy itself.

## Final decision

Deferred — flagged for the next quarterly review, targeted for September 2026. No option was selected.

## Decision owner

Harriet Doyle (as the deferral owner; no substantive decision made).

## Rationale

Considered low urgency; wanted more data before acting.

## Conditions or exceptions

None decided.

## Actions

- Victor Adeyemi to compile specific examples of manager-approved expenses over $500 ahead of the September review. No interim action taken.

## Unresolved matters

The underlying gap (managers informally pre-approving expenses above the documented threshold) remained open as of this meeting.
